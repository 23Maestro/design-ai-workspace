# Pilot brief

Status: draft. No client app is connected and no pilot has run.

## Goal from the supplied post

Keep Figma for ideas and prototypes. Put reusable React components styled with
Tailwind in a repo. Let designers direct Claude or Codex to build screens from
those components. Designers check the running result and ask for changes.
Test the proposed move of 80% of Figma work; do not report it as achieved.

## Review scope

The pilot checks 1 flow. It does not approve a full design system rebuild,
a move to Paper, or a change to current Figma workflows. Check component reuse
and design fidelity separately from behavior tests. Record engineer rework
and time spent in Figma before drawing conclusions about the workflow.

## Evidence limits

The screenshots supply the goal, not the team's app structure or results.
The 80% target is untested. No cost saving or team-scale result is claimed.
